# Missions

One view of every mission on your board, sorted by type, plus Community Goals.

## Using it

Click a category tab along the top:

**Massacre · Settlement Raids · Combat · Trade & Mining · Passenger · Covert · On-Foot Ops · Other · Community Goals**

- **Massacre** and **Settlement Raid** missions show grouped **kill-progress bars**, so you can see at a glance how
  many kills are still needed across all your missions for the same faction.
- Everything else shows **one card per mission**. Click a card for that mission's full details.
- **All Missions** opens one flat, sortable table across every category.

The Missions windows only exist while you have active missions.

**Example.** You've taken five massacre missions against the same faction from different stations. Open the
**Massacre** tab and the bars show your total progress, rather than five separate counters to remember.

## Pages

Missions has more than one page. Use the large orange **◀** and **▶** buttons at the top of the panel to move
between them.

## Settings

**Settings → Missions**: choose what shows on each card (kill progress, mission counts, a commodities-needed
summary and so on).
